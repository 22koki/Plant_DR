# 🌿 Plant Doctor AR

> **AI-powered plant health diagnosis for crops, gardens, flowers, lawns, grass, trees, and shrubs.**

Plant Doctor AR is an intelligent plant-health diagnostic platform that combines **AI, computer vision, image analysis, and trusted agricultural knowledge** to help users understand visible plant-health problems.

Users can photograph or upload images of crops, garden plants, flowers, lawns, trees, and shrubs. Plant Doctor AR analyzes visible symptoms, identifies likely causes, and provides clear treatment and prevention guidance backed by relevant sources.

---

## 🌱 About Plant Doctor AR

Plant-health problems are not always easy to identify.

A farmer may notice unusual spots spreading across tomato leaves.

A homeowner may discover brown patches appearing across their lawn.

A gardener may find that their flowers are suddenly wilting.

A landscaper may need to identify what is damaging a client's plants.

Plant Doctor AR provides an accessible first step:

> **See a problem → Scan it → Understand it → Take informed action.**

The application is designed as a professional diagnostic assistant rather than a replacement for qualified agronomists, plant pathologists, or agricultural professionals.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Our goal is to make plant-health information more accessible by combining modern artificial intelligence with reliable agricultural and horticultural knowledge.

Plant Doctor AR aims to help users:

- Detect potential plant-health problems earlier
- Understand visible symptoms
- Identify possible diseases and pests
- Investigate environmental or nutrient-related stress
- Find practical treatment information
- Learn how to prevent recurring problems
- Keep records of previous plant-health scans

Before scanning, users can specify what they are examining:

```text

🌾 Crop
🌹 Flower
```

This allows Plant Doctor AR to adapt its analysis.

For example, a lawn containing several large brown patches should not be analyzed in exactly the same way as an individual tomato leaf.

## 🌿 Plant Identification

Where image quality and available evidence allow, the system attempts to identify the plant or vegetation being examined.

Example:

```text
Plant

```

Identification should be communicated with appropriate uncertainty when the available image is insufficient.

## 🦠 Disease & Problem Detection

Plant Doctor AR investigates possible:

- Plant diseases
- Fungal conditions
- Bacterial conditions
- Pest damage
- Nutrient-related symptoms
- Water stress
- Heat stress
- Environmental stress
- Other visible plant-health abnormalities

Results are presented as **likely possibilities rather than guaranteed diagnoses**.

---

## 📊 Confidence & Uncertainty

The application communicates the confidence of an analysis.

Example:

```text
LIKELY CONDITION

Early Blight

Confidence
HIGH

Severity
MODERATE
```

When appropriate, alternative possibilities are also shown.

```text
OTHER POSSIBLE CAUSES

• Septoria leaf spot
• Nutrient deficiency
```

This helps prevent an uncertain AI result from being presented as a confirmed diagnosis.

---

## ⚠️ Severity Assessment

Visible symptoms may be classified as:

```text
MILD
MODERATE
SEVERE
```

Severity represents what can be observed from the submitted information and should not be interpreted as a complete professional assessment.

---

## 🧠 Explainable AI

Plant Doctor AR does more than display the name of a possible condition.

The application explains **why** a diagnosis was suggested.

Example:

> Brown circular lesions with darker concentric rings were detected primarily on older leaves. These visible characteristics are consistent with symptoms commonly associated with early blight.

This gives users context behind the AI's result.

---

## 🥽 Visual Symptom Highlighting

Plant Doctor AR can highlight suspicious areas directly on the submitted image.

This allows users to visually understand which areas contributed to the analysis.

Possible indicators include:

```text
🟢 Normal-looking tissue

🟡 Area requiring attention

🔴 Strongly affected area
```

Users can inspect highlighted regions for additional information.

---

## ❓ Follow-Up Questions

A photograph may not always provide enough information for a reliable assessment.

When necessary, Plant Doctor AR can ask additional questions such as:

- When did the symptoms first appear?
- Are the affected leaves mainly older or younger leaves?
- Are insects visible underneath the leaves?
- Are nearby plants experiencing similar symptoms?
- Has there recently been heavy rainfall?
- How frequently is the plant or lawn watered?
- Have any treatments recently been applied?

Answers can then be used to refine the analysis.

# 🔎 AI Research Layer

After identifying likely causes, Plant Doctor AR retrieves relevant information from trusted agricultural and horticultural resources.

The research layer supports:

- Diagnosis explanations
- Treatment recommendations
- Management options
- Prevention guidance
- Follow-up recommendations

The system should prioritize authoritative sources such as:

- Agricultural research organizations
- Government agricultural agencies
- Universities
- Agricultural extension services
- Recognized plant pathology resources
- Trusted horticultural organizations

Relevant sources should be displayed alongside important recommendations.

---

# 🩺 Treatment Guidance

Plant Doctor AR converts its findings into understandable next steps.

A treatment plan may contain:

### Immediate Actions

Steps that can safely be taken immediately.

### Management Options

Appropriate management approaches based on retrieved agricultural information.

### Monitoring

What symptoms or changes should be monitored after action is taken.

Example:

```text
WHAT TO DO NOW

1. Remove heavily affected leaves.

2. Keep foliage dry and avoid unnecessary
   overhead watering.

3. Improve airflow around affected plants.

4. Monitor nearby plants for similar symptoms.
```

Where chemical controls are discussed, Plant Doctor AR should rely on appropriate authoritative guidance and emphasize local regulations and product-label instructions rather than generating unsupported pesticide directions.

---

# 🛡️ Prevention

Plant Doctor AR can provide prevention guidance appropriate to the suspected problem.

Recommendations may include:

- Plant sanitation
- Appropriate irrigation
- Improved airflow
- Crop rotation
- Proper spacing
- Soil management
- Monitoring nearby plants
- Removing affected material
- Appropriate lawn-care practices

Recommendations depend on the plant and suspected condition.

# ⚠️ Disclaimer

Plant Doctor AR provides **AI-assisted plant-health information**.

Results are based on available images, user-provided information, AI analysis, and retrieved reference material.

Image-based analysis may be incomplete or incorrect.

Plant Doctor AR is not a substitute for professional diagnosis from a qualified agronomist, plant pathologist, agricultural extension officer, or other appropriate expert.

Serious, widespread, commercially significant, or uncertain plant-health problems should be verified by an appropriate professional.
